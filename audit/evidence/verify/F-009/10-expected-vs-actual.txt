=== STEP 10: EXPECTED VS ACTUAL (TRIPLE) FOR F-009 ===
Finding: F-009
Title: Application database role retains full UPDATE and DELETE privileges on stock movements and posted invoices without database triggers or rules

1. EXPECTED (source: MASTER_PROJECT_PLAN.md, docs/business-rules.md, docs/product-requirements.md REQ-009, REQ-036, OWASP ASVS v4.0.3 Item 8.3.1):
   Database-level controls (e.g. BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE triggers or revoked privileges) must enforce immutability:
   - Rejects all UPDATE and DELETE operations on `stock_movement`.
   - Rejects DELETE operations on `sales_invoice` when status is POSTED, PARTIALLY_PAID, PAID, or CANCELLED_BY_CREDIT_NOTE.
   - Rejects UPDATE operations on posted `sales_invoice` financial, identification, and snapshot fields, and forbids downgrading status to bypass immutability.
   - Protects lines belonging to posted sales invoices (`sales_invoice_line`) without interfering with or breaking operations on DRAFT invoices.
   - Associated regression tests must prove every trigger works and catch mutations.
