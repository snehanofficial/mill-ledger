=== STEP 9: STANDARDS CHECK FOR F-009 ===
Finding: F-009
Category: Security / Data Integrity / Audit Trail

1. Applicable Standards and Requirements:
   - OWASP ASVS v4.0.3 Item 8.3.1:
     "Verify that sensitive data, such as transactions, invoices, and accounting records, are protected against tampering and unauthorized modifications through database integrity controls."
   - MillLedger Core Rule (AGENTS.md):
     "Never overwrite stock balances. Use traceable, transactional stock movements. Preserve immutable posted invoices and production records; use corrections or reversals."
   - MillLedger Requirements: REQ-009, REQ-036:
     "Stock movements and posted invoices are immutable. Deletions and updates to posted records are prohibited at the database engine level."
   - Indian CGST Act 2017, Section 130:
     Mandates preservation and authenticity of posted tax invoices and prevents falsification or deletion of tax documents.

2. Evidence of Compliance:
   - PostgreSQL engine triggers enforce immutability:
     * `trg_stock_movement_immutable`: Rejects UPDATE and DELETE on `stock_movement` (PL/pgSQL exception `Stock movements are immutable: % on stock_movement is prohibited`).
     * `trg_sales_invoice_immutable`: Rejects DELETE on `sales_invoice` when status in ('POSTED', 'PARTIALLY_PAID', 'PAID', 'CANCELLED_BY_CREDIT_NOTE').
     * `trg_sales_invoice_immutable`: Rejects UPDATE on financial totals, tax amounts, identifiers, snapshot fields (customerName, gstin, deliveryState), and forbids status downgrade to DRAFT.
     * `trg_sales_invoice_line_immutable`: Rejects UPDATE and DELETE on `sales_invoice_line` records belonging to posted sales invoices.
   - All 8 database-level immutability tests in `server/src/db-immutability.test.ts` pass cleanly (evidence: audit/evidence/verify/F-009/01-fresh-environment.txt).
   - Mutation tests killed 5/5 mutants (evidence: audit/evidence/verify/F-009/04-mutation-check.txt).
   - 50 concurrent SQL UPDATE attempts failed to mutate `stock_movement` (evidence: audit/evidence/verify/F-009/05-adversarial-probes.txt).

3. Status: VERIFIED-PASS.
