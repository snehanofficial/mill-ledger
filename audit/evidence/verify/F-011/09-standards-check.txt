Category: Statutory Compliance / Timezone / Invoicing

1. Applicable Standards and Statutory Rules:
   - Rule 46 of Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Rules, 2017:
     Clause (b) mandates that a tax invoice must contain: "a consecutive serial number not exceeding sixteen characters, in one or multiple series, containing alphabets or numerals or special characters... unique for a financial year."
   - Section 2(7) of the Income-tax Act, 1961:
     Defines the Indian financial year as the period commencing on the 1st day of April and ending on the 31st day of March of the next year.
   - Timezone Requirement:
     The Indian Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN) and statutory books operate strictly on Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30). Cloud host server timezones (e.g. AWS/GCP default UTC) must not affect invoice serial numbering or tax year classification.
