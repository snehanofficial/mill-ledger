=== F-022 Verification: 03 Regression Reproduction ===
Finding Title: `auditService.log` is invoked only on report exports; zero audit log records are generated for orders, dispatches, production runs, invoices, or payments
Defect Analysis:
1. Operational Mutation Logging Void:
   Static analysis showed `auditService.log` was only invoked on report downloads (`server/src/modules/reporting/reporting.routes.ts:167`).
   Zero operational transactions across sales orders, dispatches, production runs, tax invoices, purchase receipts, or customer payments recorded audit log events.
2. Forensic & Statutory Repudiation Risk:
   Under OWASP Top 10 A09:2021 and ASVS 8.2, business-critical and security-relevant actions must be recorded in an immutable audit trail with actor (userId, userEmail), action, entity, entityId, timestamp, and details.
   In case of unauthorized price overrides, bogus dispatches, or fraudulent stock reversals, forensic investigation was impossible.

Fix Applied:
1. Sales Module (`sales.service.ts`):
   - `createSalesOrder`: logs `action: "CREATE"`, `entity: "SalesOrder"`, capturing orderNumber, customerId, totalAmount, status.
   - `updateDraftOrder`: logs `action: "UPDATE"`, `entity: "SalesOrder"`, capturing orderNumber, status, notes.
   - `approveSalesOrder`: logs `action: "APPROVE"`, `entity: "SalesOrder"`, capturing approving admin userId and order total.
   - `rejectSalesOrder`: logs `action: "REJECT"`, `entity: "SalesOrder"`, capturing rejection reason and admin userId.
   - `createCustomer` & `updateCustomer`: logs `CREATE` / `UPDATE` on `entity: "Customer"`.
2. Dispatch Module (`dispatch.service.ts` & `sales.routes.ts`):
   - `postDispatch`: logs `action: "DISPATCH"`, `entity: "Dispatch"`, capturing dispatchNumber, salesOrderId, salesInvoiceId, lineCount.
3. Production Module (`production.service.ts` & `production.routes.ts`):
   - `createDraftRun`: logs `action: "CREATE"`, `entity: "ProductionRun"`, capturing runNumber, status, recipeId, warehouseId.
   - `postProductionRun`: logs `action: "POST"`, `entity: "ProductionRun"`, capturing runNumber, actualGoodQty, totalRunCost.
   - `reverseProductionRun`: logs `action: "REVERSE"`, `entity: "ProductionRun"`, capturing runNumber, reversal reason.
4. Finance Module (`finance.service.ts` & `finance.routes.ts`):
   - `createInvoiceFromOrder`: logs `action: "POST"`, `entity: "SalesInvoice"`, capturing invoiceNumber, salesOrderId, customerId, totalAmount.
   - `issueCreditNote`: logs `action: "CREATE"`, `entity: "CreditNote"`, capturing creditNoteNumber, salesInvoiceId, reason, totalAmount.
   - `issueDebitNote`: logs `action: "DEBIT_NOTE_ISSUED"`, `entity: "debit_note"`, capturing debitNoteNumber, supplierId, totalAmount.
5. Payment Module (`payment.service.ts` & `payment.routes.ts`):
   - `recordPayment`: logs `action: "CREATE"`, `entity: "Payment"`, capturing paymentNumber, customerId, amount, paymentMode.
   - `processPaymentReversal` (`bouncePayment` / `reversePayment`): logs `action: "PAYMENT_BOUNCED"` / `action: "PAYMENT_REVERSED"`, capturing reversal reason, paymentNumber, reversedAt timestamp.
6. Inventory Module (`inventory.service.ts` & `purchase.routes.ts`):
   - `postPurchaseReceipt`: logs `action: "CREATE"`, `entity: "PurchaseReceipt"`, capturing receiptNumber, supplierId, warehouseId, lineCount.
7. Added Regression Test:
   - `audit/tests/f-022-regression.spec.ts` executes all 6 mutation workflows and asserts `prisma.auditLog` contains exact corresponding records with actor IDs and change details.
