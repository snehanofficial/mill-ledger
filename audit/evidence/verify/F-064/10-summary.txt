Status: VERIFIED-PASS

1. Root Cause:
`client/src/components/ui/button.tsx:8` applied the Tailwind utility class `disabled:pointer-events-none`.
This completely intercepted mouse pointer events on disabled buttons, preventing the browser from
computing and displaying the declared `cursor: not-allowed` style (defined in `index.css:298-301`)
and preventing tooltips or inspection affordances from activating on hover or focus.

2. Implementation Summary:
- `client/src/components/ui/button.tsx`:
  * Removed `disabled:pointer-events-none` from `buttonVariants`.
  * Added `disabled:cursor-not-allowed` and `aria-disabled:cursor-not-allowed`.
  * Added `disabled:active:scale-100` and `aria-disabled:active:scale-100` to prevent active compression on click.
- `client/src/pages/new-order-page.tsx`:
  * Replaced `disabled:pointer-events-none` with `disabled:cursor-not-allowed` on quantity decrease stepper button.
- `client/src/index.css`:
  * Enhanced `button:disabled`, `[aria-disabled="true"]`, and hover pseudoclasses to enforce `cursor: not-allowed !important;`.
- Verified 0 remaining occurrences of `disabled:pointer-events-none` on buttons across the entire client codebase.
