F-092 VERIFICATION SUMMARY: PUBLIC MARKETING, LANDING, CATALOG, & CONTACT
======================================================================
Date: 2026-11-24
Severity: P2 | Tier: T10 | Cluster: CLUSTER-14
Status: VERIFIED-PASS

1. Root Cause:
The application router previously nested the root route `/` directly inside `AuthenticatedLayout`.
Any unauthenticated access to the root URL immediately triggered a hard redirect to `/dashboard`,
which was intercepted by `AuthenticatedLayout` and bounced to `/auth/login`. No public B2B marketing,
product catalog, mandi gate coordinates, or intake inquiry surfaces existed anywhere in the application,
preventing prospective buyers, mandi traders, and paddy farmers from finding business credentials,
technical SKU specs, FSSAI licensing, or weighbridge intake hours without active ERP credentials.

2. Implementation Summary:
- `client/src/pages/public/public-header.tsx`:
  * Accessible responsive navigation header with skip-to-content link (`#main-content`) conforming to WCAG 2.4.1.
  * Tamil/English language switcher toggle with correct language tagging (`lang="ta"` / `lang="en"`, WCAG 3.1.2).
  * Dynamic CTA: "Staff ERP Login" for unauthenticated visitors, "Open Workspace" for authenticated users.
- `client/src/pages/public/public-footer.tsx`:
  * Semantic footer landmark (`role="contentinfo"`).
  * Full statutory disclosures: FSSAI Central License 12423001000456, GSTIN 33AAAAA0000A1Z5, ISO 22000.
  * Physical mill gate coordinates and mandi intake operating hours.
- `client/src/pages/public/landing-page.tsx`:
  * B2B agro-processing identity, 100 TPD Buhler sortexing overview, and core capability highlights.
  * Conforms to Owner Decision 2: sets `document.title` and dynamic robots meta tag `index, follow`.

- `client/src/app.tsx`:

  * Registered public routes (`/`, `/catalog`, `/products`, `/contact`, `/auth/login`) outside `AuthenticatedLayout`.
  * Made `AuthenticatedLayout` a pathless layout grouping authenticated internal ERP routes.
