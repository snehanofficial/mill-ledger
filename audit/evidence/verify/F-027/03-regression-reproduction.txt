=== F-027 Verification: 03 Regression Reproduction ===
Vulnerability / Defect:
Previously, model StockReservation in server/prisma/schema.prisma lacked an `expiresAt` column and reservation lifecycle timestamps. Stock reservations held against approved sales orders never expired, causing inventory to remain permanently locked in `status: ACTIVE` until manually rejected by an administrator or physically dispatched. Any rogue, abandoned, or stalled sales order permanently locked down mill inventory, causing other salesmen to encounter artificial stock-shortage rejections (denial of service).

Reproduction Test Suite in server/src/orders.test.ts ("F-027: enforces stock reservation TTL expiration and releases locked inventory back to available stock (OWASP A04:2021 & ASVS 13.2.2)"):
1. Initial available stock for Murukku verified before order submission.
2. Salesman submits order for 10 units.
3. Admin approves order specifying a 1-hour policy TTL window (reservationTtlHours: 1).
4. Stock reservation row is verified to be created with status ACTIVE and expiresAt approximately 1 hour in future.
5. Available stock is verified to decrease by 10 units.
6. Sweeping before expiration confirms reservation remains ACTIVE and unexpired.
7. Simulating time passage past TTL (2 hours in future) and executing sweep via POST /api/v1/sales/reservations/expire-stale transitions reservation to EXPIRED.
8. Database record confirms reservation status transitioned to EXPIRED.
9. Available stock query at simulated future time confirms the 10 units are automatically restored to available-to-promise inventory.
10. Order notes are verified to record automatic expiration event.
