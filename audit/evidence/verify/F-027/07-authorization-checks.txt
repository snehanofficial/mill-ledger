=== F-027 Verification: 07 Authorization & Business Flow Checks ===
Standard: OWASP Top 10:2021 A04:2021 (Insecure Design), OWASP API Security Top 10:2023 API6:2023 (Unrestricted Access to Sensitive Business Flows), and OWASP ASVS v4.0.3 Section 13.2.2 (Timely state transitions).

1. Expiration Sweeper Endpoint:
   - `POST /api/v1/sales/reservations/expire-stale` is protected by `requireAuth` and `authorize("order", "update")`.
   - Unauthorized roles lacking `order:update` are denied with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
2. Order Approval Authorization:
   - Only authorized roles with `order:approve` can specify TTL parameters during order approval.
3. Automated Background Sweeping:
   - Stock availability calculations automatically invoke the expiration sweeper, ensuring unprivileged callers can never benefit from or be denied stock due to expired stale reservations.
