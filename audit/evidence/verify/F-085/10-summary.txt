F-085 VERIFICATION SUMMARY: INDIC/TAMIL NUMBER FORMATTING, DATES & COLLATION
======================================================================
Date: 2026-11-24
Severity: P2 | Tier: T9 | Cluster: CLUSTER-13
Status: VERIFIED-PASS

1. Root Cause:
Financial formatters hardcoded "en-IN" with no support for "ta-IN" localization or
Unicode Collation Algorithm (UCA / UTS #10) sorting. As a consequence, toggling the application
to Tamil retained English Gregorian notation and lacked Tamil alphabetical collation.

2. Implementation Summary:
- `shared/src/formatters.ts`:
  * Upgraded `formatINR` and `formatQuantity` to accept `locale` and `lang` options ('en' | 'ta' / 'ta-IN').
  * Upgraded `formatDate`, `formatDateShort`, `formatDateTime`, `formatTime` with `FormatDateOptions`
    supporting `locale` and `lang` ('ta-IN' renders authentic Tamil month names like "ஏப்.").
  * Implemented `compareStrings` conforming to Unicode Collation Algorithm (UTS #10) with Tamil and
    English linguistic collation support.
- `client/src/hooks/use-formatters.ts`:
  * Created contextual `useFormatters` hook automatically synchronizing formatting and collation
    locale with active UI language (`language === "ta" ? "ta-IN" : "en-IN"`).
- `client/src/lib/formatters.ts`:
  * Re-exported `useFormatters` and shared formatters.
- `client/src/lib/formatters.test.ts`:
  * Added unit test assertions covering Tamil currency grouping, Tamil date months, and Tamil/English collation.
