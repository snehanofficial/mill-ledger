Remediation Details:

2. `client/src/pages/dispatch-page.tsx`:
   - Intercepted dispatch form submission with a confirmation dialog (`ConfirmationDialog`).
   - Dialog explicitly warns of permanent stock deduction consequences: "This action will permanently deduct finished goods from physical inventory and generate an immutable dispatch record... Warning: This operational action cannot be undone. Physical on-hand stock balances will be deducted immediately."
   - Displays sales order number, customer name, total packet deduction, and vehicle details before committing.
3. `client/src/pages/orders-page.tsx`:
   - Guarded sales order rejection with `ConfirmationDialog` (`role="alertdialog"`).
   - Dialog explicitly states the business consequences: "Rejecting this order will permanently cancel order {orderNumber} for {customer.name} and release all stock reservations back to inventory. Warning: This action is irreversible. The customer booking will be permanently cancelled."
   - Embeds the rejection reason input and provides clear Cancel vs Destructive Confirm Rejection actions.
