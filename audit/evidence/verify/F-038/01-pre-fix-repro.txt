Pre-fix reproduction for F-038:
====================================================================
Finding: F-038 (Suppliers Master Cannot Be Added or Managed via UI or API)
Severity: P1 | Tier: T5 | Cluster: CLUSTER-08

Observation:
1. Prior to remediation, suppliers were a static seed-only entity with no create/update API endpoints.
   Attempting to POST to `/api/v1/inventory/suppliers` or `/api/v1/purchases/suppliers` resulted in 404 Route Not Found.
2. In `shared/src/schemas/inventory.schema.ts`, schemas `createSupplierSchema` and `updateSupplierSchema` were completely missing.
3. In `server/src/modules/inventory/inventory.routes.ts`, only read-only `GET /suppliers` existed; mutations `POST /suppliers` and `PUT /suppliers/:id` were absent.
4. On the frontend, there was no `/suppliers` route, no `SuppliersPage` component in `client/src/pages/`, no navigation item under Inventory, and no link to manage suppliers from the purchases receipt interface.
5. This blocked 54 downstream findings (including P0 finding F-070).
