=== F-012 Verification: 07 Authorization & Access Control Checks ===
1. Customer Creation RBAC:
   - POST /api/v1/sales/customers requires requireAuth and authorize("customer", "create").
   - Admin is permitted; unauthenticated callers receive 401 Unauthorized; unauthorized roles receive 403 Forbidden.
2. Customer Update RBAC:
   - PUT /api/v1/sales/customers/:id requires requireAuth and authorize("customer", "update").
   - Prevents unauthorized modification of master customer tax identifiers.
3. Multi-Tenant Scoping:
   - Customer organizationId is automatically resolved from authenticated context or user tenant scope.
