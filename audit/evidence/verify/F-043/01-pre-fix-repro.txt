Finding: F-043: Pervasive Tamil Localization Failure Across Internal Business Routes Despite Language Selector
Severity: P1
Tier: T5

Pre-fix defect:
`dictionary` in `client/src/lib/i18n.tsx` had only 30 basic entries.
Across all core operational pages (`dashboard-page.tsx`, `orders-page.tsx`, `invoices-page.tsx`, `dispatch-page.tsx`, `payments-page.tsx`, `stock-page.tsx`, `purchases-page.tsx`, `production-page.tsx`, `customers-page.tsx`, `suppliers-page.tsx`, `reports-page.tsx`, `new-order-page.tsx`), zero components imported `useLanguage` or `t()`.
Furthermore, in `client/src/components/shell/authenticated-shell.tsx`, `AppNav`, `AppNavItem`, `ShellBreadcrumbs`, and user menu rendered hardcoded English strings.
When Tamil ("தமிழ்") was selected in the language switcher, over 95% of the ERP remained 100% in English, creating a barrier for mill floor operators and regional clerks.
