F-043 Verification Summary:
---------------------------
Finding: F-043: Pervasive Tamil Localization Failure Across Internal Business Routes Despite Language Selector
Severity: P1
Tier: T5
Cluster: CLUSTER-08

Root Cause:
`client/src/lib/i18n.tsx` contained an incomplete dictionary of only 30 basic terms. The translation helper was neither imported nor wired into any of the 11 core operational pages or primary sidebar/header navigation components, causing over 95% of the UI to remain strictly in English even when Tamil ("தமிழ்") was selected.

Remediation:
1. Expanded `client/src/lib/i18n.tsx` with 180+ authentic domain translations covering all ERP navigation routes, operational statuses, action buttons, table column headers, commodity/packaging entities, and KPIs.
2. Built a fast reverse-lookup index in `translate()` allowing both namespaced keys (e.g. `nav.orders`) and exact English labels (e.g. `Order number`) to resolve cleanly to regional Tamil unicode.
3. Connected `useLanguage()` and `t()` into navigation shell elements: `AppNav`, `AppNavItem`, `ShellBreadcrumbs`, `SidebarUserMenu`, `HeaderSearchButton`, and `SidebarCollapseToggle`.
4. Connected `useLanguage()` and `t()` into all 11 core operational pages:
   - `dashboard-page.tsx`
   - `orders-page.tsx`
   - `production-page.tsx`
   - `stock-page.tsx`
   - `invoices-page.tsx`
   - `dispatch-page.tsx`
   - `payments-page.tsx`
   - `customers-page.tsx`
   - `purchases-page.tsx`
   - `suppliers-page.tsx`
   - `reports-page.tsx`
   - `new-order-page.tsx`
5. Verified bilingual parity and translation integrity with `audit/tests/f-043-regression.test.ts` and `client/src/lib/i18n.test.ts`.
