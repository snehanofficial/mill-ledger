FINDING F-049 PRE-FIX REPRODUCTION
===================================
Finding ID: F-049
Title: Complete Absence of In-App Documentation, Onboarding Guidance, and Domain Jargon Tooltips
Severity: P2
Category: UX / Help & Documentation / Learnability
Standard refs: NN/g H10 (Help and Documentation), Phase 7 Specification Check 10

Observed Defect:
1. Zero in-app documentation, user guides, or operational manuals were linked in the shell header, sidebar, or user dropdown menu.
2. Complex food manufacturing and accounting domain acronyms (BOM, ATP, FIFO, Yield Variance, WAC) were presented as plain raw text headers across production, stock, reports, and payments pages without any tooltips or contextual definitions.
3. First-time users or new shop-floor operators had no in-app explanation of the sequential operational workflow (Suppliers -> Purchases -> Production -> Sales Orders -> Dispatches -> Invoicing -> Collections).
