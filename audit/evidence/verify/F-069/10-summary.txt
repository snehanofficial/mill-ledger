======================================================================
F-069 VERIFICATION SUMMARY: SCROLL RESTORATION ON MAIN CONTAINER
======================================================================
Date: 2026-11-24
Severity: P2 | Tier: T7 | Cluster: CLUSTER-11
Status: VERIFIED-PASS

1. Root Cause:
In `client/src/components/shell/authenticated-shell.tsx`, the primary scrollable
container is internal (`<main id="main-content" className="... overflow-y-auto ...">`)
rather than `window`. React Router DOM's default `<ScrollRestoration />` only monitors
`window.scrollY`, causing navigation between routes to preserve stale vertical scroll offsets
on `#main-content`, disorienting users (violating Master Rulebook Phase 9 & NN/g heuristics).

2. Implementation Summary:
- `client/src/hooks/use-scroll-restoration.ts`:
  * Created dedicated `useScrollRestoration` hook for internal scroll containers.
  * Continuously records container vertical scroll offsets keyed by `location.key`.
  * On `PUSH` or `REPLACE` navigation: Resets `container.scrollTop = 0` (or scrolls to hash anchor target).
  * On `POP` navigation (browser back/forward button): Restores the previous scroll position for that history entry immediately and on `requestAnimationFrame`.
  * Resets `window.scrollTo({ top: 0, behavior: "instant" })`.
- `client/src/layouts/authenticated-layout.tsx`:
  * Integrated `useScrollRestoration("main-content")`.
  * Preserved focus reset to `#main-content` with `{ preventScroll: true }` to avoid unexpected viewport jumps.
