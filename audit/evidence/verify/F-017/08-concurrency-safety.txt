=== F-017 VERIFICATION EVIDENCE 08: CONCURRENCY SAFETY ===
Timestamp: 2026-11-23T15:14:25+05:30
Scope: Order creation transactionality and downstream reservation atomicity

1. Order Creation Transaction Integrity:
- `salesService.createOrder` runs entirely inside `prisma.$transaction(async (tx) => { ... })`.
- Validates assigned customers, calculates statutory line taxes, creates order header, and creates all order lines atomically.
- Idempotency key prevents duplicate order creations under network retry.

2. Downstream Reservation Protection:
- By preventing unvalidated `status: "APPROVED"` during order creation, the system eliminates zero-reservation ghost approvals.
- True approvals happen exclusively inside `approveOrder()` with `FOR UPDATE` pessimistic row locks on `inventory_item` and atomic inserts into `stock_reservation`.
- Verified in `server/src/sales-concurrency.test.ts` and `server/src/concurrency-idempotency.test.ts`.
