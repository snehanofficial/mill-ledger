=== F-034 Verification: Authorization Checks ===

When authentication fails (401 Unauthorized) or authorization is denied (403 Forbidden), the error envelope reliably emits:
- 401: code="UNAUTHORIZED", message="Session required" (or specific reason), requestId="<UUID>"
- 403: code="FORBIDDEN", message="Forbidden: ..." (or specific reason), requestId="<UUID>"
Stack traces and internal role definitions are never leaked in authorization failure envelopes.
All RBAC and ABAC test suites continue to pass 100%.
