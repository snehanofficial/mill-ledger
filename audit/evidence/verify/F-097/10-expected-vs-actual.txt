=== STEP 10: EXPECTED VS ACTUAL (TRIPLE) FOR F-097 ===
Finding: F-097
Title: Architectural Inconsistency: Zero Centralized Currency & Date Formatters across 7 Financial Surfaces (Ad-hoc .toFixed(2) & Fragmented Locales)

1. EXPECTED:
   Source: MillLedger Architecture Baseline (AGENTS.md), docs/business-rules.md, and Jakob Nielsen Heuristic #4:
   - The system must adhere to the "Rule of One": one canonical money formatter and one canonical date/time formatter.
   - Indian accounting ERPs must consistently display currency formatted to Indian numbering standards (en-IN format: ₹1,00,000.00) with two decimal places (paise) across all financial surfaces.
   - Dates and timestamps must follow consistent Indian Standard Time (IST) formatting.
   - Zero ad-hoc .toFixed(2) calls or discordant locales (such as undefined or []) across financial views.

2. ACTUAL:
   - Shared canonical formatting module created in shared/src/formatters.ts and exported via @mill-ledger/shared and client/src/lib/formatters.ts:
     * formatINR: Handles numbers, strings, and Decimal objects with en-IN grouping (Lakhs/Crores), 2 decimal places, and ₹ prefix.
     * formatQuantity: Formats discrete quantities with Indian numbering and optional unit suffixes.
     * formatDate: Formats dates to Indian convention (DD MMM YYYY).
     * formatDateShort: Formats dates to DD/MM/YYYY.
     * formatDateTime & formatTime: Formats IST timestamps cleanly.
   - All 7 operational financial surfaces (invoices-page.tsx, reports-page.tsx, stock-page.tsx, purchases-page.tsx, payments-page.tsx, orders-page.tsx, production-page.tsx) and auxiliary surfaces (dashboard-page.tsx, dispatch-page.tsx, device-outbox-sheet.tsx) refactored to use centralized formatters.
   - Zero occurrences of toLocaleString() or .toFixed() remain in client/src/pages/ and client/src/components/.
   - 16/16 regression tests pass in client/src/lib/formatters.test.ts.
   - Mutation check killed 4/4 deliberate mutants (surviving mutants: 0).
   - Full regression radius passed (18/18 benchmark assertions, 10/10 SQL database invariants, 123/123 tests, 0 typecheck errors, 0 lint errors, successful production build).
   - Minor residual observation:
     * client/src/pages/payments-page.tsx:272 interpolates raw numbers in a validation toast error string:
       `Total manual allocations (₹\${totalManual}) exceed payment amount (₹\${amountNum})`
     * formatDate(0) returns "—" because `if (!date)` evaluates numeric 0 as falsy.

3. DELTA:
   - Architectural inconsistency in currency and date formatting across all financial surfaces is fully resolved.
   - All financial numbers consistently display with Indian grouping (₹1,00,000.00) and dates display in Indian format.
   - Remaining items 3 and 4 from original audit report (Dual HTTP Client and Error Envelopes) are out-of-scope for CLUSTER-01 (Financial Math) and are tracked under F-034 and HTTP security clusters.
