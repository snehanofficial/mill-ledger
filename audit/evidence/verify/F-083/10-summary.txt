VERIFICATION SUMMARY: F-083
Title: Silent Server Acceptance of Insufficient Stock Offline Orders Violating Explicit Conflict Queue Requirement (REQ-052)
Severity: P1 | Tier: T9 | Cluster: CLUSTER-13
================================================================================

1. Root Cause:
   When synchronizing queued offline orders, `client/src/offline/sync-engine.ts` made a
   POST request to `/sales/orders`. Although the backend accurately reported stock shortages
   in `response.data.shortages`, `sync-engine.ts` ignored this field and blindly marked the
   outbox entry with `status: "synced"` and `succeeded += 1`. This led salesmen to believe
   orders with substantial inventory shortages were completely fulfilled and confirmed,
   violating REQ-052 and Owner Decision #9 ("Put conflicting offline orders into a CONFLICT
   queue when stock is insufficient at synchronization time").

2. Remediation Applied:
   - Updated `client/src/offline/sync-engine.ts` in `executeSyncLoop`:
     - Inspects `response.data.shortages` returned from the sales order API.
     - If any shortage exists (`activeShortages.length > 0`), the outbox order is
       quarantined into `status: "conflict"` with `lastErrorCode: "STOCK_INSUFFICIENT"`,
       the total shortage units are formatted into `lastErrorMessage`, `conflicts` is
       incremented, and the sync loop continues (`continue`).
     - Preserves `serverOrderId` and `serverOrderNumber` on the conflicting outbox record
       so administrators or salesmen can inspect and adjust the order line items.
     - Added HTTP 409 to conflict catch block ensuring non-idempotent server conflict
       responses are also routed into the explicit Conflict Queue.
