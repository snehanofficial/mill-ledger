=== F-028 Verification: 03 Regression Reproduction ===
Finding Title: Dexie IndexedDB fails to purge sensitive customer PII and pending offline orders upon user sign-out
Defect Analysis:
1. Persistent PII & Outbox on Logout:
   In client/src/layouts/authenticated-layout.tsx, the logout routine invoked queryClient.clear() to purge in-memory React Query state, but never touched offlineDb (Dexie IndexedDB).
2. Data Leakage on Shared Devices:
   All cached customers (including customer names, phone numbers, state, GSTIN tax identifiers) and pending offline orders remained unencrypted in browser IndexedDB (mill_ledger_offline_db) across user logout and account switches. Subsequent operators on shared tablets or shop floor terminals could inspect or extract sensitive enterprise data.

Fix Applied:
- Implemented purgeOnSignOut() method in MillLedgerDatabase (client/src/offline/db.ts) executing an atomic read-write transaction clearing all 4 tables: customers, products, outbox, and meta.
- Integrated offlineDb.purgeOnSignOut() into handleSignOut in client/src/layouts/authenticated-layout.tsx.
- Integrated offlineDb.purgeOnSignOut() into authClient.signOut() in client/src/lib/auth-client.ts to guarantee purge across all logout paths.
- Verified with targeted unit test client/src/offline/db.test.ts and full test suite.
