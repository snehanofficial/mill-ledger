Date: 2026-11-24
Severity: P2 | Tier: T7 | Cluster: CLUSTER-10
Status: VERIFIED-PASS

1. Root Cause:
Dynamic shortage banners and form validation error alerts rendered conditionally
into the DOM without programmatic ARIA live region declarations (`aria-live="polite"`
or `aria-live="assertive"`) or appropriate roles (`role="status"` or `role="alert"`).
Assistive technology users (screen reader users) were not notified when stock shortages
or form validation errors appeared dynamically during order creation, approval,
production planning, dispatch, or payment recording workflows.

2. Implementation Summary:
- `client/src/components/ui/alert.tsx`:
  * Configured default `role={props.role || (variant === "danger" || variant === "warning" ? "alert" : "status")}`
  * Configured default `aria-live={props["aria-live"] || (variant === "danger" ? "assertive" : "polite")}`
- Operational pages updated:
  * `client/src/pages/orders-page.tsx`: Added `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` to order shortage banner and approval shortage notice.
  * `client/src/pages/new-order-page.tsx`: Added `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` to live catalog stepper shortage banner.
  * `client/src/pages/production-page.tsx`: Added `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` to batch carton shortage banner.
  * `client/src/pages/payments-page.tsx`: Added `role="alert"` and `aria-live="assertive"` to payment validation error banner.
  * `client/src/pages/dispatch-page.tsx`: Added `role="alert"` and `aria-live="assertive"` to dispatch validation error banner.
