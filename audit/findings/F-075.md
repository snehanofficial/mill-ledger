## Expected (Source)
- **Source**: `Problem_Statement_Winter_Internship_Application_2026.md`, `audit/01-expected-spec.md` (REQ-056, REQ-057), Owner Decision 1 & Decision 4.
- Expected design:
  1. Product variant images paired with clear text labels on sales booking screens.
  2. Images served in modern compressed formats (WebP or AVIF) with fallback.
  3. Content-hashed filenames for immutable caching (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`).
  4. BlurHash or ThumbHash stored with each image record and rendered as a placeholder to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
  5. Upload pipeline: Multer/Sharp handling resizing (variants), re-encoding to WebP/AVIF, stripping EXIF metadata, and enforcing upload limits.
  6. Below-the-fold images use `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`, while LCP image uses `fetchpriority="high"`.

## Delta
Nothing from the expected image pipeline exists today:
- No product image upload or storage mechanism.
- No WebP/AVIF variant generation.
- No EXIF stripping.
- No content-hashed image filenames for products.
- No BlurHash or ThumbHash placeholders.
- Product booking and catalog tables display text only with no product imagery.

---

## Impact
- **Who is hurt**: Field salesmen booking orders on mobile devices who rely on rapid visual product identification (e.g. distinguishing 200g vs 500g murukku pouches by packet artwork).
- **How**: Increased cognitive burden and higher rate of accidental wrong-SKU booking in noisy retail environments.
