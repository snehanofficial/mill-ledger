## Expected (Source)
- **Source**: `docs/offline-sync.md` ("Never silently discard unsynchronized orders") and `audit/01-expected-spec.md` (REQ-052).
- When a user clicks "Discard" on a pending or conflicted offline order in the Device Outbox, the application must require an explicit confirmation dialog explaining that the uncommitted order will be discarded, with options to cancel or confirm. Discarded entries must either be preserved in a recovery queue (soft delete) or at minimum require two-step affirmative confirmation to prevent accidental loss of mobile field orders.

## Delta
Clicking "Discard" executes a direct hard delete against the IndexedDB `outbox` table with **zero confirmation dialog** (no `AlertDialog`, modal, or browser confirm dialog) and **zero recovery mechanism**. A single accidental tap permanently erases customer field orders before they can ever be synchronized to the server.

---

## Impact
- **Who is hurt**: Traveling sales representatives operating mobile touchscreens in rural territories.
- **How**: Salesmen frequently operate handheld devices with one hand while on customer site. An accidental tap on the compact "Discard" button immediately deletes a ₹7,500 - ₹50,000 distributor order without recourse, resulting in lost sales, customer dissatisfaction, and unrecoverable field work.

---

## Repro Steps
1. Log in as Salesman (`salesman1@millledger.local`).
2. Disconnect device network (`navigator.onLine = false`).
3. Create an order for Retailer A (150 packets, ₹7,875).
4. Open the Device Outbox drawer by clicking the "Offline" badge in the header.
5. Tap the "Discard" button next to the queued order.
6. Observe: The order vanishes instantly with no confirmation prompt.
7. Inspect IndexedDB `mill_ledger_offline_db.outbox`: The order is completely wiped from local storage.

---

## Root Cause
- File: [`client/src/components/shell/device-outbox-sheet.tsx`](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/client/src/components/shell/device-outbox-sheet.tsx#L280)
  - The Discard button directly invokes `removeOrder(ord.id)` without wiring a Radix `AlertDialog` or modal confirmation state.
- File: [`client/src/offline/sync-engine.ts`](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/client/src/offline/sync-engine.ts#L293)
  - `removeOfflineOrder` performs a destructive `delete` instead of setting a soft-delete status (e.g. `status: "discarded"`).

---

## Recommended Fix (Do Not Apply)
1. Wrap the Discard button in an `AlertDialog` confirmation dialog (`AlertDialogTrigger`, `AlertDialogContent`, `AlertDialogAction`).
2. Display a confirmation message: "Are you sure you want to discard this offline order for [Customer Name] (₹[Amount])? This action cannot be undone."
3. Implement soft-delete in Dexie (`status: "discarded"`, `discardedAt: new Date().toISOString()`) so salesmen can undo accidental discards within 24 hours.
