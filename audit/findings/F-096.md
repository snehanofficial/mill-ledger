## Delta
- The test suite provides high nominal statement coverage by replaying the single happy-path benchmark scenario, but has massive blindspots in business rule enforcement:
  - 24 mutants survive in payment allocation (40.68% failure rate).
  - 6 mutants survive in GST calculation (27.27% failure rate).
  - Inter-state tax calculation is completely untested.

---

## Impact
- **Statutory Non-Compliance**: A developer could accidentally invert IGST calculation or break inter-state invoicing, and the entire test suite (`pnpm test` and `pnpm benchmark`) would pass with 100% green checks.
- **Financial Fraud Risk**: Stripping cross-customer validation in payment allocation would pass unit tests unnoticed, allowing rogue actors to apply payments to unrelated customer accounts.

## Root Cause
- Benchmark overfitting: The test suite was written exclusively to satisfy the exact 18 benchmark assertions in `Problem_Statement_Winter_Internship_Application_2026.md` without implementing negative, boundary, or multi-state test suites.

---

## Recommended Fix
1. Add inter-state invoice tests to `invoice-dispatch.test.ts`:
   - Create and approve an order for Distributor B in Karnataka (`state: "Karnataka"`).
   - Post invoice and assert `cgstAmount === 0`, `sgstAmount === 0`, and `igstAmount === lineTax`.
   - Assert individual line item totals (`lineTotal`).
2. Add comprehensive error guard tests to `payments.test.ts`:
   - Test allocation sum exceeding payment amount (400).
   - Test negative allocation amounts (400).
   - Test non-existent invoice ID (404).
   - Test allocating customer A's payment to customer B's invoice (400 IDOR protection).
   - Test allocating payment to a `DRAFT` or `CANCELLED` invoice (400).
