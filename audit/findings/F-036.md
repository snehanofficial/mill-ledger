# Finding F-036: Dashboard Sales Revenue Silently Drops Invoices Without Lines (SQL vs API Mismatch)

**ID**: F-036  
**Title**: Dashboard Sales Revenue Silently Drops Invoices Without Lines (SQL vs API Mismatch)  
**Severity**: P0 (Wrong money / revenue reporting)  
**Category**: Domain & Accounting Integrity  
**Standard refs**: OWASP ASVS v4.0.3 Section 10.3 (Accounting Integrity), REQ-026  

---

## 1. Expected vs Actual (E9 Triple)

- **EXPECTED (REQ-026 / Business Rules Section 4)**:
  Total revenue displayed on the executive dashboard and sales tax register must reconcile exactly with the sum of all posted sales invoice total amounts recorded in PostgreSQL:
  $$\sum \text{Invoice.totalAmount} = \text{Dashboard.totalRevenue}$$
- **ACTUAL (`audit/scripts/verify-fake-data-and-sql.ts` live execution)**:
  In PostgreSQL table `sales_invoice`, 7 invoices exist for organization `MILL` totaling **₹19,875.00** (`status != 'CANCELLED_BY_CREDIT_NOTE'`). However, `GET /api/v1/dashboard?organizationId=MILL` returns:
  `"totalRevenue": 7875`
  A discrepancy of **₹12,000.00** was observed live.
- **DELTA**:
  Underreporting of ₹12,000.00 in total revenue. `reportingService.getSalesReport` iterates exclusively over `inv.lines`:
  ```ts
  for (const inv of invoices) {
    for (const line of inv.lines) {
      grandTotal += Number(line.lineTotal);
    }
  }
  ```
  Invoices that do not have associated line items are completely excluded from `grandTotal`, despite having a valid header `totalAmount` in the database ledger.

---

## 2. Impact

- **Financial Impact**: Owners and accountants viewing the dashboard see a false revenue figure that does not match their general ledger or bank deposits.
- **Audit Failure**: External tax auditors and GST authorities comparing outward invoices against the system overview will detect a severe variance.

---

## 3. Repro Steps & Root Cause

1. Insert or generate an invoice where lines are not present or separate from header (e.g. legacy migrated data or partial receipt).
2. Query `GET /api/v1/dashboard?organizationId=MILL`.
3. Compare the returned `metrics.totalRevenue` against `SELECT SUM("totalAmount") FROM "sales_invoice"`.
4. **Root Cause**: `server/src/modules/reporting/reporting.service.ts` lines 77-85: `getSalesReport` computes revenue by summing lines instead of validating invoice headers, with zero fallback or reconciliation check when lines are absent.

---

## 4. Recommended Fix (Do Not Apply)

1. Enforce strict database constraint that posted sales invoices must have at least one line item before posting.
2. In `reportingService.getSalesReport`, calculate header totals and verify line sum equality; if an invoice has no lines, either log an integrity exception or use `inv.totalAmount` with a warning flag.

---

## 5. Regression Test to Add

- `audit/tests/verify-fake-data-and-sql.ts` automated assertion verifying `Dashboard.totalRevenue == SUM(sales_invoice.totalAmount)`.
