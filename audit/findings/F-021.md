# Finding F-021: Procurement Subsystem Item Ledger Exposed to Salesmen via Router Multi-Mount

**ID**: F-021  
**Title**: Multi-mount of `inventoryRouter` exposes `GET /api/v1/purchase-receipts/items/:id/ledger` to salesmen due to broad `inventory:read` privilege  
**Severity**: P1  
**Category**: Architecture / Authorization Leak / Separation of Concerns  
**Standard Refs**: OWASP Top 10:2021 A01:2021 (Broken Access Control), OWASP API Security Top 10:2023 API5:2023 (Broken Function Level Authorization)  

---

## Expected (Source)
Source: [docs/business-rules.md](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/docs/business-rules.md) Section 2, [docs/authorization-matrix.md](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/docs/authorization-matrix.md).

## Delta
Re-mounting the same router across two distinct operational URL prefixes (`/inventory` and `/purchase-receipts`) creates an unintended access leak where salesmen gain access to procurement item movement ledgers under the `/purchase-receipts` domain.

---

## Impact
- **Procurement Confidentiality**: Salesmen can inspect raw material suppliers, intake quantities, procurement timestamps, and underlying stock valuations that should be restricted to inventory managers and accounting.

---

## Repro Steps
1. Authenticate as `salesman1@millledger.local`.
2. Issue `GET /api/v1/purchase-receipts/items/<inventory_item_id>/ledger`.
3. Observe HTTP 200 OK returning full movement ledger history including purchase receipts and production consumption.

---

## Root Cause
- [server/src/app.ts:48](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/server/src/app.ts#L48): Mounting `inventoryRouter` at `/api/v1/purchase-receipts` exposes all inventory routes under the purchase receipts prefix.
- [server/src/modules/inventory/inventory.routes.ts:111](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/server/src/modules/inventory/inventory.routes.ts#L111): Endpoint relies on broad `inventory:read` instead of scoped `purchase:read` or separate routers.

---

## Recommended Fix
1. Separate purchase receipt endpoints into a distinct `purchase.routes.ts` requiring `purchase:read` / `purchase:create`.
2. Do not mount generic inventory ledger routes under `/api/v1/purchase-receipts`.
