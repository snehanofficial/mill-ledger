# F-084: Plaintext Sensitive Customer PII and Outbox Orders Persist in IndexedDB Across User Logout and Account Switches

- **ID**: F-084
- **Title**: Plaintext Sensitive Customer PII and Outbox Orders Persist in IndexedDB Across User Logout and Account Switches
- **Severity**: P2
- **Category**: Data Privacy / Client Storage Security
- **Standard Refs**:
  - `docs/offline-sync.md` ("Dexie/IndexedDB: user-scoped snapshots, drafts, and outbox commands")
  - OWASP Top 10:2021 A01: Broken Access Control & A02: Cryptographic Failures
  - OWASP ASVS v4.0.3 Requirement 3.5.3, 8.3 (Sensitive Data Storage on Client)
  - Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023, India) Sec 8 (Data Security & Retention)

---

## Expected (Source)
- **Source**: `docs/offline-sync.md` ("Storage: Dexie/IndexedDB user-scoped snapshots, drafts, and outbox commands") and OWASP ASVS v4.0.3.
- Upon user sign-out, all client-side cached business records containing customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) including names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, GSTIN numbers, and pricing snapshots must be cleared or encrypted with a user-derived credential to prevent data leakage on shared workplace devices.

## Delta
`offlineDb` (Dexie) is not cleared on sign-out. Plaintext customer PII (including phone numbers, tax identifiers, and order drafts) remains resident on the client machine indefinitely across sessions and user switches.

---

## Impact
- **Who is hurt**: Commercial customers whose proprietary contact and tax information is stored on shared field or factory tablets.
- **How**: In mill operations, sales tablets or floor workstations are frequently shared among rotating staff. A departing salesman or operator leaves full customer contact lists, negotiated pricing, and draft orders accessible in the browser's IndexedDB storage.

---

## Repro Steps
1. Log in as Salesman (`salesman1@millledger.local`).
2. Navigate to `/orders/new` (hydrating local customer and product tables).
3. Click user profile avatar in the header and select "Sign out".
4. After returning to `/auth/login`, open Chrome DevTools > Application > Storage > IndexedDB > `mill_ledger_offline_db`.
5. Expand `customers` table.
6. Observe: Customer names, phone numbers (`+91 98400 12345`), and GSTINs (`33AABCR5678G1Z2`) are visible in cleartext.

---

## Root Cause
- File: [`client/src/layouts/authenticated-layout.tsx`](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/client/src/layouts/authenticated-layout.tsx#L68)
  - `handleSignOut` purges only the in-memory `queryClient`, completely omitting cleanup calls to `offlineDb.customers.clear()`, `offlineDb.products.clear()`, and `offlineDb.meta.clear()`.

---

## Recommended Fix (Do Not Apply)
1. In `handleSignOut`, purge master data tables:
   ```ts
   await offlineDb.customers.clear();
   await offlineDb.products.clear();
   await offlineDb.meta.clear();
   ```
2. For unsynced orders in `outbox`, check `pendingCount`: if 0, purge `offlineDb.outbox.clear()`. If pending orders exist, warn the user and keep them encrypted under a user key.
