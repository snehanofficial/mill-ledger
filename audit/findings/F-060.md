# F-060: Missing Language of Parts (`lang="ta"` / `lang="en"`) on Multilingual Text Fragments

- **ID**: F-060
- **Title**: Missing Language of Parts (`lang="ta"` / `lang="en"`) on Multilingual Text Fragments
- **Severity**: P3
- **Category**: Accessibility / Internationalization / Pronunciation
- **Standard refs**: WCAG 2.2 SC 3.1.2 (Language of Parts - Level AA)

---

## 1. Expected vs Actual

- **EXPECTED (WCAG 2.2 SC 3.1.2 Level AA)**:
  The human language of each passage or phrase in the content must be programmatically determined, except for proper names, technical terms, words of indeterminate language, and words or phrases that have become part of the vernacular of the immediately surrounding text.
- **ACTUAL (evidence: `client/src/pages/login-page.tsx:191`, `client/src/lib/i18n.tsx:100-115`)**:
  While the root `<html lang>` attribute correctly tracks the primary application language ("en" or "ta"), inline multilingual fragments lacked `lang` tags:
  1. When the page was in English (`<html lang="en">`), the language toggle button displayed the Tamil script `"தமிழ்"` (`login-page.tsx:191`) without `lang="ta"`.
  2. In the demo role selector (`login-page.tsx:345`), Tamil descriptions and English technical abbreviations were intermixed without sub-language tagging.
  3. When the page was in Tamil (`<html lang="ta">`), English terms ("Email address", "kg", "BOM", "FIFO", email addresses `admin@millledger.local`) were presented inside Tamil sentences without `lang="en"`.
- **DELTA**:
  Screen readers with Tamil or English speech synthesizers attempt to pronounce foreign script/words using the primary document phoneme engine, resulting in garbled text-to-speech pronunciation.

---

## 2. Impact

- **Who is hurt**: Multilingual screen reader users who switch between Tamil and English voices.
- **Operational consequence**: An English synthesizer reading the string "தமிழ்" without `lang="ta"` either skips the glyphs entirely or speaks raw Unicode hex numbers ("Tau-Me-Le").

---

## 3. Repro Steps & Root Cause

### Repro Steps
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:4173/auth/login`.
2. Inspect the language toggle button:
   `<button ...>தமிழ்</button>`
   Observe: No `lang="ta"` attribute is attached to the span or button.

### Root Cause
Omitting explicit `lang` props on localized dictionary phrases, script switch buttons, and demo account metadata.

---

## 4. Fix & Verification

### Implementation
- Added `LangPart` component in `client/src/lib/i18n.tsx` to wrap foreign language fragments with explicit `lang` attribute.
- Tagged language switcher toggle buttons in `login-page.tsx` and `authenticated-shell.tsx` with dynamic `lang={language === "en" ? "ta" : "en"}`.
- Tagged demo roles phrases, account labels, emails, and footer copyright in `login-page.tsx` with `lang="en"`.
