# F-058: Absence of Confirmation and Review Steps on Irreversible Financial Transactions

- **ID**: F-058
- **Title**: Absence of Confirmation and Review Steps on Irreversible Financial Transactions
- **Severity**: P1
- **Category**: Accessibility / Error Prevention / Financial Integrity
- **Standard refs**: WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.4 (Error Prevention (Legal, Financial, Data) - Level AA)

---

## 1. Expected vs Actual

- **EXPECTED (WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.4 Level AA)**:
  For web pages that cause financial transactions or commit user data that cannot be reversed, at least one of the following must be true:
  1. **Reversible**: Submissions are reversible.
  2. **Checked**: Data entered by the user is checked for input errors and the user is provided an opportunity to correct them.
  3. **Confirmed**: A mechanism is available for reviewing, confirming, and correcting information before finalizing the submission.
- **ACTUAL (evidence: `client/src/pages/invoices-page.tsx:583-595`, `client/src/pages/payments-page.tsx:240-250`, `server/src/modules/billing/invoices.service.ts`)**:
  1. **GST Credit Note Issuance (`invoices-page.tsx:585`)**:
     Issuing a Credit Note reduces accounts receivable and legal GST tax liability with an immutable, gapless invoice sequence. Clicking the single button:
     ```tsx
     <Button
       size="sm"
       onClick={() =>
         creditNoteMutation.mutate({
           invoiceId: selectedInvoice.id,
           reason: creditNoteReason,
           totalAmount: creditNoteAmount,
         })
       }
     ```
     Immediately executes the irreversible transaction. There is no confirmation dialogue, no review summary ("You are about to issue Credit Note for ₹X.XX against Invoice INV-XXXX. Confirm?"), and the credit note cannot be deleted or undone once posted.
  2. **Payment Allocation (`payments-page.tsx:240`)**:
     Submitting payment entries posts directly to customer balances and closes invoice debts immediately without a review/confirmation summary.
- **DELTA**:
  Irreversible financial and tax ledger modifications occur on direct button click without a secondary review or confirmation safeguard.

---

## 2. Impact

- **Who is hurt**: Accounts personnel, business owners, and operators with motor or cognitive impairments prone to accidental clicks.
- **Operational consequence**: Incorrect credit note issuances distort official GST returns (GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B) and ledger balances, creating severe accounting reconciliation burdens.

---

## 3. Repro Steps & Root Cause

### Repro Steps
1. Navigate to `/invoices` as Accountant or Admin.
2. Select an outstanding invoice and click "Credit note".
3. Enter an amount and click "Issue credit note".
4. Observe that the transaction posts instantly without a confirmation prompt or review summary.

### Root Cause
Direct mutation invocation on primary button click without utilizing an `AlertDialog` confirmation pattern.

---

## 4. Recommended Fix & Regression Test

### Recommended Fix
Wrap final financial submission triggers in an `AlertDialog` (from `@/components/ui/alert-dialog`) summarizing the transaction:
"Confirm Credit Note of ₹[Amount] for Invoice [Number]? This action is permanent and recorded in the audit log."

### Regression Test
In Playwright, click "Issue credit note" -> assert confirmation dialog appears with summary before `apiClient.post` is dispatched.
