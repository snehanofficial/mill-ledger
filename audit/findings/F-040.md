# Finding F-040: Dead Links and Placeholder Pages in Primary Navigation (`/orders/new`, `/exports`, `/settings`)

**ID**: F-040  
**Title**: Dead Links and Placeholder Pages in Primary Navigation (`/orders/new`, `/exports`, `/settings`)  
**Severity**: P1 (Missing UI for required capability / Dead UI)  
**Category**: Frontend Architecture & Usability  
**Standard refs**: NN/g Heuristic #1 & #4, REQ-054, REQ-058  

---

## 1. Expected vs Actual (E9 Triple)

- **EXPECTED (UI/UX Specification Section 3 & REQ-054)**:
  Primary navigation items exposed to operational roles must navigate to functioning screens:
  - "New order" in sidebar must open the field order entry screen.
  - "Exports" in sidebar must provide options for CSV/Excel data export.
  - "Settings" in sidebar must allow organization tax, sequence, and mill configuration.
- **ACTUAL (`client/src/app.tsx:53-67` & `client/src/pages/placeholder-pages.tsx`)**:
  Clicking these primary sidebar navigation links mounts `PlaceholderPage` components:
  - `/orders/new` -> `NewOrderPage`: "New order Workspace. This view is currently scheduled for rollout in the next operational milestone."
  - `/exports` -> `ExportsPage`: "Exports Workspace. This view is currently scheduled for rollout in the next operational milestone."
  - `/settings` -> `SettingsPage`: "Settings Workspace. This view is currently scheduled for rollout in the next operational milestone."
- **DELTA**:
  Three core navigation paths exposed directly to end users lead to dead-end mock placeholders, confusing users and breaking standard navigation affordances.

---

## 2. Impact

- Salesmen clicking "New order" in the sidebar assume the system cannot create orders, unaware that order creation is buried inside a modal button on `/orders`.
- Administrators clicking "Settings" cannot manage organizational parameters, financial year series, or system users.

---

## 3. Repro Steps & Root Cause

1. Sign in as Salesman or Admin.
2. In the sidebar, expand "Orders" and click "New order".
3. Observe page renders placeholder card with badge "Module in Progress".
4. Click "Exports" in sidebar. Observe placeholder card.
5. Click "Settings" in sidebar. Observe placeholder card.
6. **Root Cause**: `client/src/app.tsx` routes lines 133, 223, 226 point to lazy imports from `client/src/pages/placeholder-pages.tsx` instead of production-ready portal screens.

---

## 4. Recommended Fix (Do Not Apply)

1. For `/orders/new`: Either redirect `/orders/new` to `/orders?action=new` or extract `NewOrderModal` into a dedicated full-page form at `client/src/pages/new-order-page.tsx`.
2. Build functional screens for `/exports` and `/settings` or remove them from navigation until implemented.
