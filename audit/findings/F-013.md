# Finding F-013: Missing Payment Reversal, Cheque/NACH Bounce and Debit Note Flows

**ID**: F-013  
**Title**: Backend lacks payment bounce/reversal methods and debit note data structures required for double-entry correction  
**Severity**: P1  
**Category**: Core Business Workflow / Finance / Statutory Accounting  
**Standard Refs**: Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (Dishonour of Cheque), REQ-037, REQ-039  
**Status**: OPEN  

---

### Expected (source)
Under [product-requirements.md](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/docs/product-requirements.md) REQ-037 / REQ-039 and [business-rules.md](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/docs/business-rules.md):
In Indian FMCG food manufacturing mills, distributor and retailer payments frequently occur via Cheque or NACH mandate. When a cheque or ECS/NACH transfer bounces:
1. The payment record must be marked as `BOUNCED` or compensating `PAYMENT_REVERSAL` transaction recorded.
2. Allocated amounts must be released back to the original invoices, restoring their `outstandingAmount` and transitioning status back from `PAID` to `POSTED` or `PARTIALLY_PAID`.
3. Debit notes must be supported for customer debit adjustments (e.g. price difference, return of goods by supplier, or dishonour charges).

### Actual (evidence links)
In [payment.service.ts](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/server/src/modules/finance/payment.service.ts):
- There is **zero** function, endpoint, or business logic for reversing a payment or recording a bounced payment. `typeof paymentService.bouncePayment` is `undefined`.
- In `schema.prisma`:
  - `PaymentStatus` enum defines `DRAFT, POSTED, CANCELLED, REFUNDED`, but `BOUNCED` or `REVERSED` is absent.
  - There is NO `DebitNote` model or table in `schema.prisma`.
- When tested in `/audit/scripts/verify-payments.ts`, once a payment is recorded, it is permanent; no compensatory rollback of allocations exists.

### Delta
If a customer's ₹50,000 cheque bounces at the bank, the accounting system cannot reverse the receipt. The invoices remain marked `PAID`, the customer's ledger shows ₹0 outstanding, and factory staff continue shipping goods to a defaulting customer.

### Impact
Financial loss and bad debt exposure. The mill cannot track dishonoured cheques, leaving receivables understated on owner and accountant dashboards.

### Repro Steps
1. Grep for bounce/reversal in `server/src/modules/finance/`:
   `grep -inE "bounce|revers" server/src/modules/finance/payment.service.ts`
2. Command returns exit code 1 (no matches).
3. Script execution: `pnpm --filter @mill-ledger/server exec tsx ../audit/scripts/verify-payments.ts`.

### Root Cause
`PaymentService` in `server/src/modules/finance/payment.service.ts` only implemented `recordPayment`, `getPayments`, `getCustomerLedger`, and `getReceivablesSummary`.

### Recommended Fix (do not apply)
1. Add `bouncePayment(paymentId, reason)` to `PaymentService` that atomically sets payment status to `BOUNCED`, deletes/reverses `PaymentAllocation` records, and adds back allocated amounts to each `salesInvoice.outstandingAmount`.
2. Add `DebitNote` model to Prisma schema with corresponding `financeService.issueDebitNote()`.

### Regression Test to Add
`audit/tests/payment-bounce.test.ts` creating an invoice, paying it in full, invoking `bouncePayment()`, and asserting invoice status reverts to `POSTED` with original outstanding balance.
