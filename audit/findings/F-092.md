## Recommended Surfaces (With Rationale - Do Not Implement)
1. **Public Landing Surface (`/`)**:
   - *Contents*: Mill identity (e.g. Modern Rice Mill / Food Processing Facility), operational capacity, sortex grading technology, quality certifications (FSSAI license, ISO 22000), and dual CTAs: "B2B Wholesale Inquiries" and "Staff ERP Login".
   - *Rationale*: Establishes business legitimacy for mandi traders, banks, and institutional buyers without exposing private ledger data.
2. **Public B2B Product Catalog (`/products` or `/catalog`)**:
   - *Contents*: Non-confidential SKU listings (e.g., Ponni Boiled Rice, Sona Masoori Raw Rice, Broken Rice 100%, De-oiled Rice Bran, Rice Husk), standard bag sizes (25kg, 50kg), moisture/purity specs, and bulk supply terms.
   - *Rationale*: Captures wholesale organic search queries while keeping client-specific price lists strictly inside the authenticated ERP.
3. **Mandi Intake & Mill Gate Contact Surface (`/contact` or `/mandi`)**:
   - *Contents*: Mill gate location coordinates, weighbridge operating hours, paddy procurement contact for local farmers, GSTIN, and inquiry form.
   - *Rationale*: Essential for LocalBusiness schema, Google Maps local search visibility, and farmer intake coordination.
4. **Public Authentication Gateway (`/auth/login`)**:
   - *Contents*: Dedicated login interface with clean semantic landmarks, password assistance link, remove hardcoded demo seed credentials from production builds, and explicit `index, follow` or `noindex` policy aligned with corporate IT standards.
