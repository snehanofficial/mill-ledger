# Finding F-010: GST Tax Rounding Discrepancy via Unrounded JavaScript Floating-Point Arithmetic

**ID**: F-010  
**Title**: Invoice tax and totals calculated with unrounded JS float math produce 1-2 paise rounding drift in 46.4% of invoices  
**Severity**: P1  
**Category**: Compliance / Financial Precision / Statutory Invoicing  
**Standard Refs**: Section 170 of CGST Act, 2017 (Rounding of tax), Rule 46 of CGST Rules, 2017, REQ-034  
**Status**: OPEN  

---

### Expected (source)
Under Section 170 of the CGST Act, 2017 and standard Indian accounting practice, line taxes must be computed with exact decimal arithmetic rounded to 2 decimal places (paise) using Half-Up rounding (`ROUND_HALF_UP`). Individual CGST, SGST, and IGST line amounts must sum precisely to invoice tax totals (`totalTaxAmount = cgstAmount + sgstAmount + igstAmount`), and `totalAmount = taxableAmount + totalTaxAmount`.

### Actual (evidence links)
In [finance.service.ts:116-160](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/server/src/modules/finance/finance.service.ts#L116-L160), line taxes and header totals are computed using native JavaScript IEEE 754 floating-point operations (`Number(line.approvedQuantity) * Number(line.approvedUnitPrice)`, `lineTax / 2`) with **zero line-level rounding**:
```typescript
const lineTaxable = qty * price;
const lineTax = (lineTaxable * taxRate) / 100;
const lineTotal = lineTaxable + lineTax;
```
When tested against an independent statutory Decimal implementation across 1,000 randomized invoice configurations in `/audit/scripts/verify_rounding_1000.py`:
- **464 out of 1,000 invoices (46.4%)** exhibited rounding discrepancies of 1 to 2 paise.
- Sample Test 4: App computed total ₹18,347.93 (CGST ₹1021.16, SGST ₹1021.16, Tax ₹2042.32), whereas statutory Decimal half-up computed ₹18,347.95 (CGST ₹1021.17, SGST ₹1021.17, Tax ₹2042.34).
- Evidence artifact: [/audit/evidence/rounding-discrepancy-1000.txt](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/audit/evidence/rounding-discrepancy-1000.txt).

### Delta
Sub-paisa fractional amounts accumulate during floating-point loops, causing invoice tax totals and grand totals to diverge from line-level rounded components and causing GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B statutory filing mismatches.

### Impact
Statutory filing rejection on the GST portal (gst.gov.in) due to mismatch between line tax components and summary tax totals. Audit queries from state and central tax authorities for discrepancies between electronic cash ledger credits and outward tax invoices.

3. Review sample test 2 (14 pkts @ ₹54.21 @ 18% GST): App total is ₹895.55, Reference total is ₹895.54.

### Root Cause
[finance.service.ts:116-160](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/server/src/modules/finance/finance.service.ts#L116-L160) performs float math on converted `Number()` values without applying `Decimal.ROUND_HALF_UP` to two decimal places on each line item.

### Recommended Fix (do not apply)
Refactor `finance.service.ts` to use `Prisma.Decimal` or `decimal.js` throughout invoice posting, rounding each line's taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, and line total to 2 decimal places with `Decimal.ROUND_HALF_UP`.
