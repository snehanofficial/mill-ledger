# F-106: Lack of Data Protection Policies, Retention Schedules, Erasure/Anonymization Routines, and Subject Access Export Under DPDP Act 2023

- **ID**: F-106
- **Title**: Lack of Data Protection Policies, Retention Schedules, Erasure/Anonymization Routines, and Subject Access Export Under DPDP Act 2023
- **Severity**: P2
- **Category**: Data Protection & Privacy Compliance
- **Standard Refs**:
  - Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023, Act No. 22 of 2023, Sections 11 & 12)
  - Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act 2017, Section 36 - Retention of Accounts)
  - ISO/IEC 27701:2019 (Privacy Information Management)

---

## Expected (Source)
- **Source**: DPDP Act 2023 & Master Rulebook (Section 8):
  - Systems processing personal data in India must support statutory Data Principal rights:
    1. **Right to Access Information / Data Portability (Section 11)**: Facility to export personal data in a structured, commonly used machine-readable format upon request.
    2. **Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12)**: Procedure to delete or anonymize personal data when the purpose of collection is fulfilled, or consent is withdrawn.
    3. **Retention & Disposal Schedule**: Formal reconciliation between statutory tax retention (Section 36 CGST Act requires keeping invoice and ledger books for 72 months / 6 years from the due date of annual return) and personal data minimization (purging unused contact lists, former salesman session logs, and IP history).

---

## Actual (Evidence Links)
1. **No Data Deletion or Anonymization Mechanisms**:
   - `Customer` table ([`server/prisma/schema.prisma:145-165`](file:///home/snehan/projects/web/mill-ledger/server/prisma/schema.prisma#L145-L165)) holds names, phone numbers, email addresses, and GSTINs.
   - Foreign key constraints on `SalesOrder`, `SalesInvoice`, and `Payment` models have default `Restrict` behaviors. Attempting to delete a customer who has transactional history results in an unhandled database error (`P2003 / foreign key constraint violation`).
   - There are **no anonymization procedures** (e.g. masking name, removing phone/email, or archiving party records) in any service or repository.
2. **No Data Subject Export Facility**:
   - Neither the API (`server/src/modules/`) nor the UI contains an endpoint or screen to export all personal data associated with a specific customer or salesman upon request.
3. **Indefinite PII Retention Without Disposal Schedules**:
   - Salesman login records, session tokens (`session` table), audit trail IPs (`audit_logs`), and customer contact numbers are retained in perpetuity with no automated cleanup jobs, TTL triggers, or retention policies documented in the repository.

---

## Delta
- The application stores personal data indefinitely with no capabilities for customer data subject export, erasure, anonymization, or automated retention management.

---

## Impact
- **Statutory Non-Compliance**: Under Section 33 of the DPDP Act 2023, failure to observe duties of Data Fiduciaries (including processing erasure requests and data principal access) can attract statutory penalties up to ₹250 crore.
- **Data Sprawl & Privacy Exposure**: Storing historical personal contact details and user session trails indefinitely without purges inflates database storage and increases blast radius in the event of a breach.

## Root Cause
- The application was built as an MVP shop floor ledger without considering data privacy legislation or formal data retention lifecycles.

---

## Recommended Fix
1. Draft a formal Data Retention Policy in `docs/data-retention-policy.md`:
   - Mandate 72-month retention for financial records under CGST Act Section 36.
   - Mandate 90-day retention for session logs and authentication audit IP records.
2. Implement an Anonymization / Erasure service:
   - For customers with transactional history, redact personal identifiers (`phone = NULL`, `email = NULL`, `name = "ANONYMIZED_CUSTOMER"`) to satisfy erasure without breaking foreign key ledger integrity.
3. Implement a Data Subject Export endpoint (`GET /api/v1/customers/:id/data-export`) returning customer profile and related document summaries in JSON format.
